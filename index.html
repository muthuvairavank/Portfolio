02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

A designer’s eye.
A developer’s mindset.

I started with software development. Along the way, I became more interested in the decisions people make on a screen — what catches their attention, what slows them down, and what helps them move forward.

That curiosity brought me to UI/UX design. With Bavora, I explored a simple question: how can we make travel packages easier to compare?

I enjoy organizing messy information, sketching flows, and refining the small details in Figma. I’m looking for a team where I can contribute, learn from feedback, and keep growing.
